Company Overview

Tarlanacer company was established in 2025 in the commercial heart of Eurasia (Moscow) with the aim of developing a sustainable supply chain in agriculture and livestock.

Our core focus is the direct export and import of various grains and animal feed. By eliminating unnecessary intermediaries, continuously monitoring shipment quality, and fully adhering to contractual commitments, we provide a secure and transparent platform for commercial partners and bulk buyers.
